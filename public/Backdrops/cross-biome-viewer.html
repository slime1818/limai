Reference

All six biomes at 1024×768 native (fal.ai UI flux-2-pro). Framing positions: Apu+Puna LEFT (cliff/outcrop), Yungas RIGHT (tree trunk, 1st mirror variation), Selva TOP (canopy, 2nd variation — first TOP framing), Paracas BOTTOM (curved-arc dune — first BOTTOM framing), Pacifico BOTTOM (wave formation — second BOTTOM, materially distinct water vs sand). Selva sky-subject has a murky warm-brown jungle river (differentiates from Paracas coastal desert and Pacifico ocean). Pacifico is a 4-layer composite with boats as a dedicated parallax-independent layer (sky-subject + plateau + boats + framing-waves). Boats offset (0, -60) and framing-waves offset (0, +60) clear the middle zone per layer-stack y-shift diagnostic. Plateau top alpha-ramp (0.4 -> 1.0 over y=0..40px) bridges the sky-subject exit to plateau top palette seam. No wildlife cross-project. Production composite = raw painterly WebP q90; F6 gradient applied at runtime by the Next.js frontend layer in Phase 2+.
